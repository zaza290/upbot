# StudyBot AI & Review Analytics

An easy-to-use AI dashboard that helps schools and teachers quickly analyze student feedback, track ratings, and get instant summaries using AI.

---

## Who is this app for? (Target Users)

* **Teachers & Instructors:** To see what students love or complain about in their classes without reading hundreds of comments manually.
* **Department Heads & Deans:** To check overall subject ratings and spot problem areas across the school.
* **School Quality Teams:** To quickly generate feedback reports for subject improvements.

---

## What problem does it solve?

* **Old Way:** Reading thousands of student comments in Excel spreadsheets takes hours or days.
* **StudyBot Way:** AI reads all the comments in seconds, gives you a clear summary, and lets you chat directly with your feedback data.

---

## Key Features

1. **Live Scores & Charts:** Instantly see total reviews, average ratings, and red alerts for bad reviews.
2. **Easy Filters:** Filter feedback by rating score (1–5 stars) or by subject (like *Computer Science* or *Math*).
3. **1-Click AI Summary:** Click one button to get a plain-English AI summary of student feelings and complaints.
4. **Color-Coded Comments:** Reviews are color-tagged so you can quickly see praise (Green), neutral comments (Yellow), and urgent complaints (Red).
5. **Chat with StudyBot:** Ask questions in natural English (e.g., *"What are the main complaints in Python class?"*) and get instant answers.

---

## Built With

* **Frontend:** Streamlit (Python)
* **Charts & Data:** Pandas & Plotly Express
* **AI Model:** Llama 3.2 (via Hugging Face API)
